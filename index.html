My Story

Turned my passion for coffee into a personal business as a love letter for the community...

I am a mother to two wonderful boys, and my journey into the world of coffee began as a simple desire to create a warm and inviting space where families like mine could gather and enjoy quality brews. What started as a small coffee stand in my neighborhood has blossomed into Bean Haunted Coffee, a place where every cup tells a story of dedication, love, and community spirit.
